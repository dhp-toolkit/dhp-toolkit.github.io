The Digital History Pedagogy Toolkit is an open-access collection of tutorials and practical workflows for teaching and learning digital history, with particular attention to Middle Eastern history and area studies. Designed for students and instructors without extensive technical backgrounds, the toolkit connects historical questions with accessible digital methods and provides step-by-step resources that can be adapted for research and classroom use.

The tutorials introduce practical approaches to managing digital research materials, working with tools such as GitHub and Jupyter, preparing and analyzing historical texts, working with geographic data, and accessing digital historical sources. Wherever possible, the materials use examples relevant to Middle Eastern history while encouraging critical reflection on how digital tools shape the sources, methods, and interpretations of historical research.
